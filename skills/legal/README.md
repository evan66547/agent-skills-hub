# Legal / 法律工作流

Skills for **document and evidence workflows** in Mainland China legal practice support—demand letters, contract-related drafting aids, and case-folder hygiene. They guide agents with checklists and confirmation gates; they are **not** a law firm or a court.  
本分类面向中国大陆法律实务辅助中的**文书与证据工作流**（催款函、合同相关起草辅助、案卷整理等）。Skill 提供清单与确认闸门，**不是**律所或法院。

## Shared expectations / 共同约定

- Prefer contract text over screenshots for rights and obligations.  
  权利义务以合同原文优先于截图。  
- Split evidence-confirmed / user-confirmed / inferred facts before writing.  
  写入前区分证据确认、用户确认与推算事实。  
- Never commit real client files, accounts, or phone numbers to the skill repo.  
  勿将真实客户材料、账号、电话提交进 Skill 仓库。  
- Not a substitute for licensed legal advice.  
  不替代执业律师意见。
